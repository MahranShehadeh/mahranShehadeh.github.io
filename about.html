About

An unconventional path to biophysics.

I am a physicist working in single‑molecule biophysics. I completed my Ph.D. at the Weizmann Institute of Science with Prof. Gilad Haran, building plasmon‑enhanced single‑molecule FRET to resolve structural transitions and dynamics in proteins.

My route here was not the usual one. I started with a B.Sc. in Physics and Materials Science & Engineering at the Technion, moved into high‑energy physics for my M.Sc. on the ATLAS experiment at CERN, then spent a year in quantum optics studying light‑matter coupling in Bose–Einstein condensates — before turning that toolkit on single molecules.

That path left me comfortable across the whole stack: fabricating the nanostructure, aligning the optics, and writing the inference code that turns a noisy photon stream into a defensible claim. I care about reproducible analysis, open‑source tooling, and clear scientific communication.
